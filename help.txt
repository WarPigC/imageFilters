
Guide to ImageFilters.

Command format: <file path> <flag> <extra flag relevant parameter>

Available flags:
    
    -b: Brightness. Provide a 3rd argument for the change in brightness. 1 to 255 results in increase while -1 to -255 results in a decrease in the brightness intensity        

    -i: Invert. Inverts an image's colors

    -g: Grayscale. Turns a colorful RGB image to grayscale.
